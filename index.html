Turns follow a small, smooth arc. Particles never stop during a round. Your trail has a limited length: its tail disappears as you move, reopening routes. The boundary only starts closing after 40 seconds. Keep an escape route toward the center.

Pickups affect whoever collects them. L+ adds 180 to your trail; L- removes 180 (limits: 180–1100). S+ boosts speed by 35%; S- reduces it by 30% for 10 seconds. Speed effects replace each other; another pickup refreshes the timer. Pickups return after 5 seconds. Your trail and speed reset each round.

First team to three round wins. Simultaneous crashes are a draw. In co-op, at least one teammate must survive both bots. All trails are solid, including your teammate's.

Each view fills its half of the screen. Steer with the circle at the bottom of your view. Its thumb marker follows your finger while your particle stays visible above the controls as the larger arena scrolls around it. Drag to the edge of the circle for maximum speed. The corner radar shows the full map, every trail and your camera window. Face-to-face rotates each view and radar toward its player, keeping the steering circles near opposite ends of the phone. Turn it off for side-by-side keyboard play. Arenas rotate between rounds.
